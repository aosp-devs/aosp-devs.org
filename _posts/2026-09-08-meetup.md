The topic for the next AOSP and AAOS meetup is

## How to join?

This is the first meetup that we are organizing without hosting it on
meetup.com. You don't have to register upfront. You only have to join the
provided Jitsi meetup link when the online meetup starts. We will share the
link on

* our [discord server](/) in the `#events` channel and
* on the page [The September 2026 Meetup](/meetup/2026-09-meetup.html)

before the meetup starts.
