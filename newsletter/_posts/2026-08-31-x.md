## Conferences in September and October

Short heads up for the conferences. In September there will be the IndiaFOSS in
Bengaluru, India. In October there will be the Open Source Summit Europe and
the Linux Plumbers Conferences in Prague, Europe and next.app/droidcon in Berlin,
Europe.

The schedules are available. Check out the links below in the calendar.
