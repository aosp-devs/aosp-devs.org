## AOSP and AAOS Meetup in September

In September we had [The September 2026 Meetup](/meetup/2026-09-meetup.html).
It was a discussion round about LLMs and agentic tools to develop the AOSP. Quite
a lot of good points were raised and ideas shared. E.g. how to sync between
locally attached devices via adb and the agent and an AOSP build running on a
build server.

If you are interested, the recording is available on the page [The September 2026 Meetup](/meetup/2026-09-meetup.html).

## Two things from the fediverse

The C-based Linux kernel binder driver is dropped from the kernel ([Source](https://androiddev.social/@jstultz@hachyderm.io/117270740289540530)).
It's fully replaced by the rust-based binder driver:

> ~17 years after landing in staging, it looks like its time to say goodbye to the binder.c driver: https://lore.kernel.org/lkml/rm-c-binder@google.com/
>
> Kudos to to both Alice Ryhl for her huge efforts on the Rust Binder, and Carlos Llamas for his maintaiance of the C driver and his eager help with the transition!

GrapheneOS on non-public APIs ([Source](https://grapheneos.social/@GrapheneOS/117282123437636721)):

> Android 17 QPR1 is the first release since Android Honeycomb (3.x) adding new APIs for app developers without a release to the Android Open Source Project. The new APIs are currently exclusive to the Pixel OS and aren't available to other Android OEMs.
