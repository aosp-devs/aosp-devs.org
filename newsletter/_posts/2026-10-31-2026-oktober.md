## Upcoming devroom at FOSDEM

There will once again be a *FOSS on Mobile* devroom at FOSDEM'27.

Please submit your proposals for AOSP, AAOS and other mobile-related topics.
See the [CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/hyperkitty/list/fosdem@lists.fosdem.org/thread/NSCBBGPD2HPXTDOSMU2NUP37RWLH2D3Y/) for details.

> This devroom is about Free and Open Source Software running on mobile
> devices, which includes not just smartphones but any devices that you
> normally carry about with you. We are also including vehicles, which
> tend to run software derived from smartphones. We are looking for
> contributions relating to Android and AOSP related distributions, Linux
> based distributions like Sailfish and Ubuntu Touch, and non Linux
> operating systems such as OpenHarmony
>
> With the fast-growing adoption of AI models, the landscape of FOSS
> development on mobile has shifted with many new paradigms and
> challenges. How to maintain and foster the ethos of the ecosystem under
> the tremendous impact of these new trends has become an at stake topic.
>
> We aim at bringing together mobile app developers, OEMs, custom ROM
> builders, and app platform providers to combine efforts for a healthy,
> open, and sustainable ecosystem through research, experimental projects,
> and new forms of collaboration.
